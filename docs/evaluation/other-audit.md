`Other` holds **80 of 255** catalogue prompts (31.4%) and is the least
reusable category in the evaluation. Every one of them was read; this report
tests whether the families I found in them are real or invented.

## The test applied to each proposal

A cluster is only worth a category if its members resemble each other more than
they resemble the catalogue at large. `inside` is the mean pairwise similarity
within the cluster, `outside` the mean similarity between its members and every
other prompt. Prompts repeated verbatim across UC campuses are excluded from
`inside`, since seven copies of one prompt would report near-1.0 cohesion and
prove nothing.

## Would a new category actually improve recommendations?

Cohesion says a family exists; it does not say adopting it helps. Promoting a
cluster to a primary category gives its member pairs the 25-point primary
factor they currently score zero on. This counts how many pairs inside each
cluster are close enough that those 25 points would carry them over a band
boundary - the only mechanism by which a taxonomy change improves advice.

Scored exactly as matching.ts would, but with the cluster as a shared primary.
Pairs of prompts repeated across UC campuses are excluded.
